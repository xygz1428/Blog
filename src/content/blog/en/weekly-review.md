Most weekly reviews will turn into a "running account + self-blame", and then give up after persisting for three weeks.

The problem is: **Review does not produce decisions**.

## four questions

Spend twenty minutes every week answering only these four questions:

### 1. What did you actually do this week?

It’s not what you planned to do, it’s what you actually did. List specific things, not feelings.

```
✗ 「这周比较忙，效率不高」
✓ 「改完了 A 模块，B 卡在数据格式上，读了 2 篇论文」
```

### 2. What took longer than expected? Why?

This question is the core. Exceeding expectations reveals an **error in judgment** - you thought it was simple.

Common reasons:

| reason | How to change it next time |
|---|---|
| Underestimating the complexity of dependencies | Spend ten minutes first to figure out the dependencies |
| Not counting debugging time | Multiply by 1.5 when estimating |
| interrupted | Reserve a buffer, don’t fill it up |

### 3. What is the most important thing for next week?

Choose just one.

If you list five "most important things", it means there is no focus.

### 4. What should you stop doing?

This question is the easiest to skip, but the most valuable.

- an item that is no longer valuable
- An inefficient process
- An action that is just habitual

**A “Stop List” changes results more than a “To-Do List”. **

## Why do most reviews fail?

**Failure Mode 1: Only record without adjustment**

I wrote a long paragraph about "What I learned this week" and my behavior remained completely unchanged next week. The review became a diary.

**Test method**: Is there at least one item in next week's plan that has been changed due to review? If you don’t have it, it’s all in vain.

**Failure Mode 2: Excessive self-blame**

"It's not finished this week" - and then get stuck in the mood. The object of review is **process**, not character.

The correct way to ask: Is the estimate wrong, the priorities wrong, or the plan itself unrealistic?

**Failure Mode 3: The granularity is too fine**

Recording what was done every hour of every day is high cost and low value. The granularity of a week is just right—long enough to see patterns, short enough to remember clearly.

## a template

```markdown
## 2026-W46

### 实际完成
- 

### 超预期的
- 事：…… 
- 原因：……
- 下次：……

### 下周最重要的一件事
- 

### 停止做
- 
```

It can be written in five lines or less. I can’t write down the explanation and the notes are too detailed.

## When should you change the disk replication frequency?

- **The project enters the sprint period**: Change to five minutes a day, just look at "Where are you stuck today"
- **No progress for a long time**: Change to monthly to see a larger model
- **Just changed the environment**: Changed to daily, because there is new information every day

Frequency is for decision-making. **If the review cannot change next week's actions, the format should be changed. **

## summary

| question | output |
|---|---|
| what was actually done | factual baseline |
| What exceeded expectations | Judgment Calibration |
| The most important thing next week | priority |
| stop doing | Subtraction |

The value of review is not in "recording the past", but in "changing the future"**.
